# HAHA — Narrative & Video Script

> **HAHA — Humans Are Hard to Automate**
> Tell a joke that fools the AI and get paid.

Dokumen ini berisi narasi resmi HAHA dalam gaya profesional dan minimalis: dari masalah, ke alasan HAHA hadir, hingga endgame. Teks narasi ditulis dalam bahasa Inggris karena audiensnya global. Catatan produksi ditulis dalam bahasa Indonesia.

---

## 1. Manifesto (narasi utama)

**The problem**

Artificial intelligence has read almost everything humanity ever wrote.
It can pass exams, write code, and summarize a library in seconds.

But the most human things were never written down.

The pause before someone says *"I'm fine."*
The reason a joke lands in one room and dies in another.
The difference between *"sure"* and *"sure."*
What everyone at a dinner table knows, but no one says.

Machines have learned our words.
They have not learned what we mean.

This is not a small gap. It is the gap between an assistant and a companion, between a tool that answers and a system that understands. And the data needed to close it is rare, expensive, and almost impossible to manufacture. It lives only in people.

**Why HAHA**

HAHA turns that gap into a game.

Anyone can take the stage and tell the AI something only a human would get: a joke, a line with a double meaning, a message with something unsaid, a moment from real life that doesn't follow the rules.

If the AI misses it, and a crowd of real people agrees, you get paid.

Every attempt, won or lost, goes into the Joke Vault: an open, growing record of human nuance, verified by humans. Every miss teaches our AI, the Straight Man, something it didn't know. Each season, it gets harder to fool.

The value is shared. Trading fees fund the humans who beat the machine. The data is licensed to those building the next generation of AI, and the people who created it are part of the story, not raw material for it.

**The endgame**

HAHA's goal is not to prove that AI is foolish.
It is to make AI less foolish, with people, not around them.

We are building the world's largest verified record of what it means to be human: humor, feeling, context, and common sense. A dataset no lab can scrape, because it has to be earned.

And one day, the Straight Man will finally get the joke.

On that day, the people who taught it will have been paid for it.

**Humans are hard to automate. That's the point.**

## 3. Arah visual (style guide video)

**Mood:** tenang, jujur, hangat, sedikit melankolis di awal, lalu ringan dan penuh harapan. Bukan hype crypto, bukan futuristik neon.

**Gaya visual**
- **Dokumenter, footage nyata.** Kamera handheld, cahaya alami, grain halus. Wajah orang biasa dari berbagai negara dan usia: pasar, kereta, dapur, kantor, jalanan kota di malam hari.
- **Momen kecil manusia.** Tatapan sebelum tertawa, senyum yang dipaksakan, dua sahabat yang tertawa tanpa perlu bicara, orang tua yang membaca pesan lalu terdiam.
- **Robot sebagai "the Straight Man".** Satu karakter robot/humanoid berwajah datar yang mengamati manusia: duduk di bangku penonton comedy club, melihat orang tertawa di taman, menatap layar berisi pesan "I'm fine." Ia mencoba memahami, tapi selalu sedikit tertinggal.
- **Tipografi minimal.** Teks putih di atas latar hitam atau di atas footage, satu kalimat per layar, font sans-serif bersih, banyak ruang kosong.
- **Warna:** netral dan hangat (hitam, putih tulang, amber lampu panggung). Aksen satu warna brand HAHA saja.

**Audio**
- Piano atau ambient minimalis. Gunakan jeda dan keheningan sebagai elemen.
- Suara asli dari footage (tawa, suara kota) muncul pelan-pelan.
- Voice-over: suara tenang, dewasa, tidak terburu-buru. Atau tanpa voice-over, cukup teks di layar.

**Yang dihindari**
- Grafik harga, candle chart, roket, "to the moon", janji keuntungan.
- Menyebut atau menampilkan logo perusahaan AI lain.
- Wajah tokoh publik atau orang nyata yang bisa dikenali tanpa izin (gunakan talent dengan release form atau stock footage berlisensi).
- Robot yang menyeramkan atau "AI jahat". Straight Man harus terasa polos dan simpatik.

## 6. Catatan produksi

- **Footage:** kombinasi stock footage berlisensi (untuk momen dunia nyata) dan pengambilan gambar sendiri untuk adegan comedy club.
- **Straight Man:** bisa dibuat dengan CGI, robot/humanoid sewaan, atau aktor dengan kostum/makeup minimalis. Desainnya harus orisinal, jangan meniru robot dari film atau brand terkenal.
- **Talent:** gunakan release form untuk semua orang yang wajahnya terlihat jelas.
- **Bahasa:** buat versi subtitle Inggris dan Mandarin sejak awal (sesuai target pasar).
- **Kepatuhan:** jangan menyebut harga token, potensi keuntungan, atau angka valuasi di video. Penyebutan pembayaran cukup "get paid" / "paid in USDG". Tambahkan tautan ke aturan program di caption.
- **CTA caption:** "Take the stage at [domain]. Rewards vary and depend on the Tip Jar. Not financial advice."
